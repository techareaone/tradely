01 Games

Five games.
All live data.

Every game pulls from real-world sources — stock prices, live news, actual company fundamentals. No simulated numbers.

🕯️ Live Markets

CandleDojo

See the first half of a real stock or crypto chart. Predict whether price went UP or DOWN in the hidden half. Points for correct calls.

  • — Real Yahoo Finance OHLCV data
  • — Multiple timeframes: 1H, 4H, Daily, Weekly
  • — Auditable seeds — every round reproducible
  • — 60-second timer, continuous play
  • — Volume bars + full wick rendering
📈 Simulated Trading

TradeRun

Trade a real stock across 12 candles in ~60 seconds. Buy and sell shares with a live balance. Beat the baseline to earn a bonus point.

  • — 12 candles, one every 5 seconds (~60s total)
  • — BUY & SELL buttons — long-only
  • — Starts with 3× the stock price as cash
  • — +1 pt for positive return · +1 bonus to beat baseline
  • — Auto-advances between runs non-stop
📰 Live News

NewsTru

A fresh headline from major outlets every 6 hours. An AI generates a factual question from it. Type your answer — synonyms count.

  • — 5 RSS feeds: BBC, Reuters, NYT, Guardian, NPR
  • — AI-judged answers — no rigid string matching
  • — 90-second answer window
  • — Per-user caps to prevent repeated questions
  • — Rolling 6-question pool — always fresh
📊 Daily Puzzle

Tradle

A trading-themed Wordle. 6 tries to guess the daily 5-letter market word. Win, then choose to bank your points or risk them Double-or-Nothing.

  • — One word per day — same for all players
  • — Letter-by-letter colour feedback (Wordle style)
  • — Win in 1 guess → +6 pts, win in 6 → +1 pt
  • — Double-or-Nothing: stake wins for 2× next day
  • — Progress auto-saves — pick up where you left off
💹 Daily Stock Puzzle

Stockle

Guess the daily mystery stock ticker in 6 tries. Each guess reveals Market Cap, Revenue, Sector, and Country hints — hot or cold style.

  • — One stock per day — same ticker for everyone
  • — Market Cap & Revenue: % difference + ↑↓ direction
  • — Sector: exact match or miss
  • — Country: exact · same region · different region
  • — Live data from Yahoo Finance
02 Commands

Simple to run.
Easy to moderate.

/play sends a link to the game on gamely.tradely.dev — scores sync back automatically. Mods get leaderboard controls and per-game toggles.

Player commands

All game commands are slash commands. Type / in any channel where Gamely is present to see the full list.

# Quick start
/play game:CandleDojo
→ sends a link to play at gamely.tradely.dev
/play game:{...} Sends a link to play on gamely.tradely.dev. Choose CandleDojo, TradeRun, NewsTru, Tradle, or Stockle — scores sync back to Discord automatically. Everyone
/status [enable|disable] [game] View current game status for this server. Mods can enable or disable individual games or all gameplay globally. Everyone
/leaderboard action:{here|off} Post a live leaderboard in this channel — updates automatically every 30 seconds. Use off to remove it. Mod
/multiplier View the current active point multiplier for your server. Multipliers are granted via the Gamely console. Mod
/premium Manage premium features for your server. Coming soon. Mod
/servers List all Discord servers using Gamely. Hidden servers are shown as "🙈 Hidden Server" to protect their privacy, unless the command is run from that server itself. Mod
/hide [hide|show] Opt your server in or out of the public Gamely server listing at gamely.tradely.dev/servers/. hide removes it from the directory; show restores it. Platform admins can apply a permanent hide that server admins cannot reverse. Admin
