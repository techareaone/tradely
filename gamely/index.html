Games

Gamely ships with three polished mini-games, each pulling live real-world data so no two rounds are ever the same.

🕯️ Live Markets

CandleDojo

See the first half of a real stock or crypto chart. Predict whether price went UP or DOWN in the hidden half. Points for correct calls, nothing lost on misses.

  • ✓ Real Yahoo Finance OHLCV data
  • ✓ 5 timeframe options: 1H, 4H, Daily, Weekly
  • ✓ Auditable seeds — every round is reproducible
  • ✓ 60-second round timer, continuous play
  • ✓ Volume bars + wick analysis
📈 Live Trading

TradeRun

Trade a real stock across 12 candles in ~60 seconds. Buy and sell shares with a live balance, then see how your return stacks up against the seed's baseline.

  • ✓ 12 candles, one every 5 seconds (~60s total)
  • ✓ BUY & SELL buttons — long-only, no shorting
  • ✓ Starts with 3× the stock price as your balance
  • ✓ +1 pt for positive return · +1 bonus to beat baseline
  • ✓ Auto-advances to the next run after results
📰 Live News

NewsTru

A fresh headline hits every 6 hours from BBC, Reuters, NYT, Guardian and NPR. An AI generates a factual question from it. Type your answer — synonyms and paraphrases count.

  • ✓ 5 major RSS feeds, refreshed every 6 hours
  • ✓ AI-judged answers — no rigid matching
  • ✓ 90-second answer window via modal popup
  • ✓ Per-user caps to prevent repeats
  • ✓ Rolling 6-question pool — always fresh
